    A SMALL FIELD GUIDE

    Readable records. Explicit boundaries.

    Follow a field

    Use /content or /meta/status to choose a value. A missing field, a JSON null, and a duplicate name are different states. The empty pointer selects the whole record.

    Keep the source

    No data leaves this page. No analytics or browser storage. Save a project before closing or refreshing. Opening a project rebuilds the inspection.

    Know the limits

    For small files, read-only. Not a streaming viewer, editor, repair tool or secret scanner. No comments, trailing commas, compressed files, JSONPath or numeric comparisons. Full limits and setup.
